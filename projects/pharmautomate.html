Pharm’automate Pharmacy Robot

An autonomous pick-and-place pharmacy robot that reads a prescription from a QR code, navigates to the right shelf, identifies the medicine by colour, and delivers it — the whole pharmacy workflow with no human in the loop.

What I built

Built the complete robot from end effector to navigation. The QR-code-driven workflow lets a customer drop a prescription, the robot decodes it, plans a navigation route, identifies the requested medicine on the shelf via colour segmentation, picks it with a custom gripper, and returns it to the dispense bay.

Architecture highlights

  • End effector: Custom gripper designed for the medicine-bottle form factor.
  • Vision: HSV-space colour segmentation for medicine identification on the shelf.
  • Navigation: Mobile base running ROS move_base with a known map of the dispensing area.
  • Prescription input: QR-code parsing maps a customer prescription to a target medicine ID and shelf location.

Tech stack

  • ROS
  • move_base
  • OpenCV
  • HSV segmentation
  • QR codes
  • Custom gripper
  • C++
  • Python

Outcome

  • Complete autonomous pharmacy workflow demonstrated end-to-end.
  • Prescription decoded, medicine identified, picked, and delivered without human intervention.
