Autonomous Emergency Braking

Sensor-fusion AEB system that closes the loop between perception, decision, and brake actuation in 100 ms — designed for ASIL-B compliance with full FMEA coverage.

What I built

Led development of an AEB pipeline that took raw multi-sensor data, fused it through tracking and time-to-collision math, and reached the brake actuator through the vehicle gateway. The car has to know there's a collision coming, decide it should brake, and physically brake — all inside the time it takes a human to flinch.

Architecture highlights

  • Sensor fusion: Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR point-cloud processing, Delphi ESR radar tracking, and Mobileye camera object lists, fused into a unified track list.
  • Decision layer: Time-to-Collision computation with a 100 ms decision-latency budget end-to-end.
  • Vehicle integration: CAN-bus interface designed with Vector CANalyzer / CANoe. J1939 and OBD-II parsing. Brake-by-wire actuation through the vehicle gateway.
  • Safety case: Full FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) for ASIL-B compliance.
  • On-road testing: Managed a test program on Honda CR-V and Kia Soul platforms. ROS bag recording pipeline captured synchronized multi-sensor data at 2 GB/min.
