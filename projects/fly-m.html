F.L.Y.-m — Phone Drop Protection

A mini quadcopter phone case that detects a free-fall through its onboard IMU and deploys thrust-vectoring brushless motors mid-air to stabilize the phone before it hits the ground.

What I built

The phone's IMU is constantly running. We treated a 0.3 g acceleration reading as the trigger for a free-fall event — the moment a hand lets go. Four miniature brushless motors fire up and thrust-vector to either right the phone or slow its descent.

Architecture highlights

  • Free-fall detection: IMU-based threshold trigger at 0.3 g.
  • Actuation: Four brushless motors with thrust-vectoring control to right the phone in flight.
  • Emergency deployment: Sub-second motor spin-up from trigger to active thrust.

Tech stack

  • IMU
  • Brushless motors
  • Thrust vectoring
  • Embedded C
  • 3D printing

Outcome

  • Received NYU Makerspace prototype funding.
  • Working hardware demonstrator with reliable free-fall trigger.
