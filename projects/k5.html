K5 5th Generation Robot

A two-wheeled self-balancing security robot deployed from NYPD Times Square to VA Medical Centers nationwide. 100+ robots in production, 3M+ patrol hours, $20M+ revenue, four-year client retention.

What I built

I architected and managed the complete product lifecycle for Knightscope's flagship platform — from a clean-sheet redesign through full production replacement of the legacy fleet. The system runs indoors and outdoors, balances dynamically, talks to a cloud fleet-management plane, and stays online 24/7 across hundreds of customer sites.

Architecture highlights

  • Navigation stack: Led the ROS 1 to ROS 2 migration. AMCL with particle-filter optimization, DWA local planner, costmap2d with inflation layers, and Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR for ±5 cm indoor positioning.
  • SLAM: Gmapping and Cartographer for real-time map building. Loop-closure detection, scan-to-map matching, and pose-graph optimization. Multi-floor deployments with automatic map switching on elevator detection.
  • Balance control: Cascaded PID for the self-balancing platform — 1 kHz IMU inner loop, 100 Hz velocity outer loop. Complementary filter fused IMU with wheel encoders.
  • Fleet telemetry: MQTT pipeline streaming 50+ sensor parameters at 10 Hz to AWS IoT Core. Grafana dashboards for real-time fleet monitoring. Predictive maintenance via motor-current anomaly detection cut incidents 53%.
  • Computer vision: OpenCV license-plate recognition, dlib HOG+SVM face detection, and FLIR Lepton thermal anomaly detection.
  • Production scale: Replaced 100% of the legacy fleet. Owned full lifecycle — design, production, deployment.
