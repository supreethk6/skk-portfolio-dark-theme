Synthetic Data Object Detection

A detector trained almost entirely on data the world doesn't actually have to exist for — 100K+ images rendered from a game engine with domain randomization, validated against real-world KITTI at 92% mAP.

What I built

Real labelled driving data is expensive. I built a synthetic-data pipeline that pulled scenes from the GTA V engine under randomized lighting, weather, camera angle, and traffic density, generated bounding-box labels automatically, and fed them to a Faster R-CNN. The model transferred to the KITTI benchmark with 92% mAP — competitive with detectors trained on real data alone.

Architecture highlights

  • Synthetic data generation: Game-engine rendering with programmatic scene control. Domain randomization on lighting, weather, camera placement, and traffic density.
  • Data augmentation: 100K+ labelled images produced through the augmentation pipeline.
  • Detector: Custom Faster R-CNN trained on the synthetic set, evaluated zero-shot on KITTI.
  • Validation: 92% mean Average Precision (mAP) on the KITTI benchmark.

Tech stack

Outcome

  • 92% mAP on the KITTI object-detection benchmark.
  • 100K+ labelled training images produced without manual annotation cost.
  • Demonstrated viability of game-engine-based synthetic data for production detectors.
