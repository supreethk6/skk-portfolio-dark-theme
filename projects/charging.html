Autonomous Charging System

A multi-phase precision docking sequence that lets the robot fleet charge unattended — the single capability that turns "autonomous patrol" into "24/7 autonomous patrol with less than 5% downtime."

What I built

Docking is the part of the autonomy stack where many platforms quietly require human intervention. I designed a three-phase docking pipeline that combines long-range LiDAR-based dock detection with close-range fiducial alignment and a final mechanical handshake — so the robot can return to base, align, mate, and resume patrol without anyone touching it.

Architecture highlights

  • Coarse approach: LiDAR-based dock detection using RANSAC plane fitting on a known dock geometry. Robust across lighting and weather variation where camera-only approaches falter.
  • Fine alignment: AprilTag fiducial markers for sub-centimeter pose. Solves the long-tail of edge cases where LiDAR resolution alone isn't enough.
  • Final insertion: LiDAR + IR proximity + contact sensors confirm successful mating before charging begins.
  • Power management: Intelligent charging scheduler balances patrol coverage against battery state. Battery management system integration covers per-cell voltage, temperature, and state-of-charge.

Outcome
