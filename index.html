Augmenting law enforcement & public safety

Systems I’ve architected now patrol malls, hospitals, corporate campuses, and NYC’s busiest subway station. Deployed robots operate at roughly $9 per hour providing 24/7 AI-powered surveillance — a force multiplier extending police capabilities where human resources are constrained.

Protecting critical infrastructure & defense assets

The K7 platform was explicitly designed for military bases, airports, and power installations. The U.S. Air Force selected the program for an SBIR contract to reimagine base security — autonomous perimeter patrol reducing risk to human guards while providing persistent surveillance with AI threat detection.

Ensuring U.S. independence from foreign robotics technology for critical security applications.
